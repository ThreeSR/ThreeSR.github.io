Submit a paper

Preview of the submission flow:

  1. Paste an arXiv link and pick a category. Code link, training data and benchmarks are optional.
  2. We open a pull request with the entry filled in from arXiv.
  3. A maintainer reviews and merges it, and the page updates on its own.

Submissions open when the list moves to its permanent home. While you wait, a star helps us a lot.
