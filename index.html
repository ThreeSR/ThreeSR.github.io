Hello World

My name is Rui Sun (Rui is pronounced as Ray). I am a third-year CS PhD student at UCLA NLP advised by Prof. Kai-Wei Chang. My experience and research topics can be found in Experience section.

Recently, I have been mainly working on the full stack of multimodal agents: benchmark [Embodied Web Agents], agent harness design [OpenVLHarness], data generation and trajectory synthesis, then SFT and RL training [UniSearcher, ARMAP], and inference-time improvement [ToolSmith, ARMAP, WorldMind]. My work covers domains including multimodal search agent [OpenVLHarness, UniSearcher, ToolSmith], agentic text-to-image generation [UniSearcher], and computer use & embodied agents [Embodied Web Agents, WorldMind, ARMAP].

Eariler, I worked on general vision language understanding and visual commonsense reasoning [IdealGPT, GENOME, UniFine, JourneyBench, Visual Commonsense Grounding]. You can find more below.
